# Support merchant-controlled payment accounts

V1 will direct Customers to an Organization-controlled GCash, Maya, or bank account and will not hold or process funds. This preserves the product's bring-your-own-wallet wedge for businesses avoiding gateway onboarding, while requiring ownership attestation, clear recipient identity, provider-limit disclosures, and language that avoids implying official payment-provider integration.
